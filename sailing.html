US AQI

What the numbers mean

  • 0–50 Good: a great day on the water.
  • 51–100 Moderate: fine for most; sailors with heart or lung conditions should take it easy.
  • 101–150 Unhealthy for sensitive groups: shorter sails, more breaks, check in with staff.
  • 151+ Unhealthy: sailing may be paused. Watch for updates from the club.
